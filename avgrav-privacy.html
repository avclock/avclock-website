Privacy Policy — AvGrav

Last updated: October 16, 2026

AvGrav is designed to respect your privacy. The app makes no network requests at all, has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and does not transmit any personal or device information to us or to any third party. Every calculation happens entirely on your device.

Information we collect

Through the App: none. AvGrav does not know who you are, has no server, and has nothing to send your data to even if it wanted to.

Through the website and email: the AvGrav pages on avclockapp.com keep anonymous visit counts, and if you email us we receive your message. Both are described below.

Calculations and history

Every reading you calculate — API gravity, temperature, fuel type, volume, description, and any edit history — is stored only on your device, in a container shared between the app and its Home Screen widget or Watch complication on that same device. It is never sent to us or to any server. The one place it goes is your own paired Apple Watch, described below. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it (unless you've backed up your device via Apple's own iCloud/Finder backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy practices, not ours).

The Watch app

History syncs between the iPhone app and the Apple Watch app on your own paired Watch, using Apple's WatchConnectivity, which sends it directly between your two devices. There is no server in between and nothing goes to us. Photos are not synced: they stay on the device that took them. Each device stores its copy the same locally-only way described above.

Camera and photos

If you choose to attach a photo of a hydrometer, gauge, or other physical reading as proof alongside a calculation, the app requests camera access only at that moment, and only to let you take that photo. Photos you choose from your library are selected through Apple's system photo picker, which does not give the app direct access to your photo library. Either way, the photo is saved only in the same on-device, app-only storage described above — never uploaded or transmitted.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a report, it goes only where you send it, through Apple's share sheet: Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, a printer, or another app you choose. That destination's own privacy policy applies from there. Reports can include your readings, notes, and photos, so check what's in one before you send it.

Purchases

AvGrav is a paid download processed entirely by Apple. All payment processing is handled by Apple; the app never sees or stores your payment details.

This website

avclockapp.com, which hosts the AvGrav pages, blog, and web calculator, keeps simple, anonymous visit counts so we can see which pages are useful. For each event, it stores only a running total and the time it last happened, grouped by:

  • the page viewed, and whether it was scrolled to the end
  • the referring website's domain (for example google.com), or "direct"
  • the domain of an outside link that was clicked, and, for App Store discount-code links, the app and code (for example avclock/MILITARY)
  • clicks on the share button and uses of the interactive demos, such as the web calculator (the numbers you type into it stay in your browser and are not sent or stored)

The site uses no cookies, no advertising or third-party analytics, and no fingerprinting, and it does not store your IP address, browser details, or anything else that identifies you or links one visit to another. Requests from obvious automated crawlers are ignored. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes connection data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Third-party sites you reach from links on this site, including referral links, have their own privacy policies.

Email and support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply, help you, and improve AvGrav. We don't add you to any mailing list, and we don't share your email with anyone except as the law requires. We keep messages only as long as they're useful for that purpose, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Our email is provided by Proton, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Because the App sends us nothing, there is no App data on our side to access, correct, or delete; everything it stores is on your own device, and deleting the App deletes it. The website's counts are anonymous and can't be tied to you. For anything else we hold, which is only email you've sent us, you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or stop using it, or to give you a copy, by emailing avclock@protonmail.com. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Legal bases (EU and UK). Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we keep website counts on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding which pages are useful, in a way designed not to identify anyone, and we handle email on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. You can object at any time, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

U.S. state privacy laws. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for profiling or targeted advertising, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. Because we don't track visitors across sites, there is nothing for a Global Privacy Control signal to switch off, but we treat it as a valid opt-out request all the same.

Where information is processed

We operate from the United States. Website requests and email are handled by Cloudflare and Proton, which may process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards.

Security

We keep what we hold to a minimum, which is the best protection there is. We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The App and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is material, for example if the App ever began sending information anywhere, we'll describe it clearly here and in the App's release notes, and the App may ask you to review it, before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: avclock@protonmail.com.
