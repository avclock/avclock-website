TIME ZONES · NOVEMBER 2026

Winter UTC Offsets at 22 Busy Airports, Until the Clocks Change

4 min read

The clocks have settled. Europe fell back on October 25 and the United States and Canada on November 1, so the northern hemisphere is on standard time and the gaps between cities hold steady for the next four months. Here are the UTC offsets at 22 busy airports for the winter, the gaps between the city pairs people ask about most, and the dates in spring when it all moves again.

The Next Odd Weeks

  • US and Canada: Sunday, March 14, 2027. Each time zone springs forward at 2:00 a.m. local time.
  • UK and Europe: Sunday, March 28, 2027. Everyone on European time moves at 01:00 UTC.
  • Sydney and Auckland: Sunday, April 4, 2027. They fall back an hour as their summer ends.

The fall mismatch lasted one week. The spring one lasts two: from March 14 to March 27, North America has already moved and Europe hasn't, so transatlantic gaps are an hour shorter than the table above. If you have a standing call or a regular flight across the Atlantic, that's the fortnight to double-check. Our fall clocks-change guide walks through what tends to go wrong in a week like that.

Places That Never Move

Phoenix, Honolulu, Mexico City, São Paulo, Istanbul, Dubai, Delhi, Singapore, and Tokyo keep one offset all year. Their gap to you changes only when your own clocks do. Zulu doesn't move either, which is why aviation writes its schedules, weather, and NOTAMs in it. Zulu time explained has the longer version.
