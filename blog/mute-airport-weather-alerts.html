Mute Weather Alerts, Airport by Airport

2 min read

Weather Alerts (Premium) notify you when a saved airport's flight category changes: VFR sliding to MVFR, IFR moving in, that kind of thing. Useful, but until now it was all-or-nothing: turn the feature on, and you'd get a notification for every airport on your list, whether you actually cared about that one or not. If you track a dozen airports for reference but only fly out of two of them, that's a lot of noise for not much signal.

The fix: a toggle per airport

Open any saved airport's detail screen and scroll to the Weather section: there's now a "Weather Alerts for [code]" toggle right there, specific to that one airport. Flip it off, and that airport stops sending notifications while every other saved airport keeps working exactly as before.

It defaults to on for every airport, so nothing changes for you unless you actually go turn one off. The master Weather Alerts switch in Settings still gates the whole feature: flipping an individual airport's toggle doesn't do anything if that master switch is off to begin with.

This only covers weather category alerts. FAA ground-stop and delay notifications are a separate alert type and aren't affected by this toggle. If you want to quiet those too, that's the separate Delay Alerts switch in Settings.
