Why AvGrav exists

A field-ready tool for fuelers, technicians, and QA personnel

AvGrav is the modernized, field-ready API fuel gravity calculator designed specifically for aviation line service technicians, FBO operations, and quality assurance personnel. Frequently asked questions

Does the formula change per fuel type?

Not by the fuel type you pick. All four are refined products, so they all use the standard refined-products table (Table 6B). That table sets how much a fuel expands with heat by its density, in four bands: gasolines like 100LL and mogas, a narrow transition band, jet fuels, and heavier fuel oils like diesel. AvGrav picks the band from the reading itself, the same way the printed tables do. The fuel type you pick sets the typical range the result is checked against.

Is this a certified measurement instrument?

No. AvGrav is a reference and convenience tool, not a substitute for a calibrated physical hydrometer, thermometer, or your organization's official fuel-acceptance procedures. Always confirm results against official procedures before an acceptance or rejection decision.

Is this a subscription?

No. AvGrav is a single one-time purchase. No recurring charges, ever.

Does AvGrav collect my data?

No. AvGrav makes no network requests at all. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no server. Every calculation and every photo stays on your own devices; History syncs directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch, and nowhere else.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes, a full Watch app with its own calculator, history, and a complication that launches straight into a new reading. History syncs between the Watch and your iPhone, so a reading taken on either shows up on both.
