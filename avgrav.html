Frequently asked questions

Does the formula change per fuel type?

No — the correction math is the same for every fuel type in the app. Jet A, 100LL, Mogas, and Diesel are all light, refined petroleum products, so they all use the same standard correction table. What changes per fuel type is the normal reading range, not the equation.

Is this a certified measurement instrument?

No. AvGrav is a reference and convenience tool, not a substitute for a calibrated physical hydrometer, thermometer, or your organization's official fuel-acceptance procedures. Always confirm results against official procedures before an acceptance or rejection decision.

Is this a subscription?

No. AvGrav is a single one-time purchase. No recurring charges, ever.

Does AvGrav collect my data?

No. AvGrav makes no network requests at all. There are no accounts, no analytics, and nothing is ever transmitted anywhere — every calculation and every photo stays on your own device.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes, a full Watch app with its own calculator, history, and a complication that launches straight into a new reading. It currently keeps its own separate history from the iPhone app.
