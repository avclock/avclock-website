San Diego (SAN) is on Pacific Time: UTC−8 (PST) in standard time and UTC−7 (PDT) while daylight saving time is on. The airport is in San Diego, the United States. Its codes are SAN (IATA, the one on your boarding pass) and KSAN (ICAO, the one pilots and air traffic control use).

Other airports on the same clock: Los Angeles (LAX), Las Vegas Harry Reid (LAS), Seattle-Tacoma (SEA), San Francisco (SFO), Vancouver (YVR).

Zulu Time and SAN

To get local time from Zulu, subtract 8 hours in standard time. With daylight saving time on, to get local time from Zulu, subtract 7 hours. Going the other way, from local time to Zulu, do the opposite.

For example, a METAR or flight plan time of 1500Z is 7:00 a.m. local at SAN in standard time.

Common Questions

What time zone is SAN airport in?

San Diego Airport (SAN) uses the America/Los_Angeles time zone, Pacific Time. That's UTC−8 in standard time and UTC−7 in daylight saving time.

Does SAN change its clocks for daylight saving time?

Yes. The next change is on Sunday, November 1, 2026.
