Rome Fiumicino (FCO) is on Central European Time: UTC+1 (CET) in standard time and UTC+2 (CEST) while daylight saving time is on. The airport is in Rome, Italy. Its codes are FCO (IATA, the one on your boarding pass) and LIRF (ICAO, the one pilots and air traffic control use).

Other airports on the same clock: Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS), Frankfurt (FRA), Munich (MUC), Madrid-Barajas (MAD), Barcelona (BCN).

Zulu Time and FCO

To get local time from Zulu, add 1 hour in standard time. With daylight saving time on, to get local time from Zulu, add 2 hours. Going the other way, from local time to Zulu, do the opposite.

For example, a METAR or flight plan time of 1500Z is 4:00 p.m. local at FCO in standard time.

Common Questions

What time zone is FCO airport in?

Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO) uses the Europe/Rome time zone, Central European Time. That's UTC+1 in standard time and UTC+2 in daylight saving time.

Does FCO change its clocks for daylight saving time?

Yes. The next change is on Sunday, October 25, 2026.
