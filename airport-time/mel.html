Melbourne (MEL) is on Australian Eastern Time: UTC+10 (AEST) in standard time and UTC+11 (AEDT) while daylight saving time is on. The airport is in Melbourne, Australia. Its codes are MEL (IATA, the one on your boarding pass) and YMML (ICAO, the one pilots and air traffic control use).

Other airports on the same clock: Sydney (SYD).

Zulu Time and MEL

To get local time from Zulu, add 10 hours in standard time. With daylight saving time on, to get local time from Zulu, add 11 hours. Going the other way, from local time to Zulu, do the opposite.

For example, a METAR or flight plan time of 1500Z is 1:00 a.m. local at MEL in standard time, the next day.

Common Questions

What time zone is MEL airport in?

Melbourne Airport (MEL) uses the Australia/Melbourne time zone, Australian Eastern Time. That's UTC+10 in standard time and UTC+11 in daylight saving time.

Does MEL change its clocks for daylight saving time?

Yes. The next change is on Sunday, April 4, 2027.

How do I convert Zulu time to local time at MEL?
