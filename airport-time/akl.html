Auckland (AKL) is on New Zealand Time: UTC+12 (NZST) in standard time and UTC+13 (NZDT) while daylight saving time is on. The airport is in Auckland, New Zealand. Its codes are AKL (IATA, the one on your boarding pass) and NZAA (ICAO, the one pilots and air traffic control use).

Zulu Time and AKL

To get local time from Zulu, add 12 hours in standard time. With daylight saving time on, to get local time from Zulu, add 13 hours. Going the other way, from local time to Zulu, do the opposite.

For example, a METAR or flight plan time of 1500Z is 3:00 a.m. local at AKL in standard time, the next day.

Common Questions

What time zone is AKL airport in?

Auckland Airport (AKL) uses the Pacific/Auckland time zone, New Zealand Time. That's UTC+12 in standard time and UTC+13 in daylight saving time.

Does AKL change its clocks for daylight saving time?

Yes. The next change is on Sunday, April 4, 2027.
