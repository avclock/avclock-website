FAQ

Why doesn't the corrected result exactly match my physical reference wheel?
AvGrav computes the correction from the published API MPMS Chapter 11.1 refined-products table (Table 6B), including the standard adjustment for a glass hydrometer, and is checked against published table values. A printed table rounds to its own steps and a physical wheel has its own reading error, so expect differences of a few hundredths. If a result looks off, double-check both rather than assuming either tool is right, and always confirm against your official procedures.

Some of my older readings say "Recheck." What does that mean?
Early versions of AvGrav over-corrected for temperature, so readings taken well above or below 60°F came out too low or too high. That's fixed. Readings saved before the fix keep their saved value, and History shows the same reading run through the current correction next to it, so you can see exactly what changed.

Does a reading calculated on the Watch show up on my iPhone, or vice versa?
Yes. History syncs directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch, with no server in between. Photos stay on the iPhone that took them. Starred readings are never deleted by a delete on the other device.

Can I use a density hydrometer instead of API gravity?
Yes. Choose "Density kg/m³" above the reading field (or in Settings). AvGrav corrects the reading to 15°C, the way Jet A-1 is certified outside the US, and shows the API at 60°F as well.

Why did my total weight change?
A meter that isn't temperature-compensated reads the fuel's actual volume, which is larger when the fuel is warm. AvGrav now corrects that volume to 60°F before working out the weight, and shows the net 60°F volume. If your meter is already temperature-compensated, turn on "Already corrected to 60°F."

Why does the app ask for camera access?
Only if you choose to attach a photo of a hydrometer, gauge, or other physical reading as proof alongside a calculation. It's entirely optional, and the photo is only ever stored on your own device.

How do I get the app on another device?
AvGrav is a one-time paid download, not a subscription. Reinstalling it on any device signed into the same Apple Account you originally purchased with is free through the App Store.

Is AvGrav affiliated with Gammon Technical Products or any other equipment manufacturer?
No. AvGrav implements the publicly published API MPMS Chapter 11.1 correction formula, a public reference standard. It's an independent product, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any standards body or equipment manufacturer.
