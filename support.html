FAQ

Why doesn't an airport show weather?
Weather (METAR/TAF) is only available for airports with a reporting station: generally those with an ICAO code. Many small fields don't report.

Is the data official?
No. Airport data comes from OurAirports (public domain) and weather from NOAA. It is for reference only. Always confirm with official sources (FAA, NOTAMs, official charts, and ATC) before any aviation decision.
